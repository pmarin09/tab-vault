HOW IT WORKS

Three specific things, not a feature wall.

Everything else follows from these.

01

Never closes what's open

Restore opens saved tabs alongside whatever you're already doing. It never replaces or suspends your current windows.

02

One bad save, one snapshot

Every snapshot is stored on its own, not folded into a shared file. A failed write can damage at most one of them.

03

The index heals itself

What's tracked is checked against what's actually on disk every time Chrome starts, so the record can't quietly drift.
